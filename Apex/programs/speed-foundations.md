# Speed Foundations Program

**Duration:** 6 weeks  
**Sessions/Week:** 3  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Equipment:** Bodyweight  
**Goal:** Master acceleration mechanics and build sprint foundation

---

## Week Overview

| Week | Theme | Focus |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Base Mechanics | A-skip, posture, arm drive |
| 2 | Arm Drive & Posture | Coordination, body lean |
| 3 | Ground Contact | Quick feet, reactive drills |
| 4 | Acceleration Builds | Start positions, drive phase |
| 5 | Speed Endurance | Longer sprints, repeatability |
| 6 | Testing & Consolidation | Time trials, skill lock-in |

---

## Week 1: Base Mechanics

### Session A - Acceleration Emphasis (~35 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**
- Dynamic hip openers: 60s
- A-skips: 2 × 20m
- Leg swings: 30s each leg

**Drills (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| A-skip progression | 3 × 20m | 60s | Knee drive, rhythm |
| Wall lean starts | 4 × 10m | 90s | Forward body angle |

**Power (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Two-leg bounds | 3 × 6 | 90-120s | Ground force |

**Sprint Work (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Standing starts | 6 × 20m | 2.5-3 min | Acceleration |

**Cooldown (5 min)** - Hip flexor stretch, quad stretch

---

### Session B - Technique Focus (~30 min)

**Warmup (6 min)**
- Jog 200m
- Dynamic stretches

**Drills (20 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Marching A's | 3 × 20m | 45s | Posture awareness |
| Arm swings (stationary) | 3 × 30s | 30s | Arm mechanics |
| Falling starts | 4 × 15m | 60s | Lean and go |

**Cooldown (4 min)**

---

### Session C - Power Development (~35 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**
- Skipping variations
- Ankle circles

**Reactive (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Ankle pogos | 2 × 20 | 45s | Quick ground contact |
| Squat jumps | 3 × 5 | 90s | Vertical force |

**Strength (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Split squats | 3 × 8 each | 60s | Single-leg base |

**Sprints (8 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Build-up sprints | 4 × 30m | 2 min | Smooth acceleration |

---

## Week 2: Arm Drive & Posture

### Session A
- A-skips: 3 × 25m
- Arm drive drills: 4 × 15s
- Wall starts: 5 × 10m
- Bounds: 3 × 8
- 20m accelerations: 6 × (rest 2.5 min)

### Session B
- Marching drills with arm focus
- Partner arm resistance: 3 × 20s
- Posture holds: 3 × 30s

### Session C
- Pogos: 2 × 25
- Tuck jumps: 3 × 5
- Bulgarian splits: 3 × 6 each
- 25m sprints: 5 ×

---

## Weeks 3-6: Condensed

**Week 3 - Ground Contact**
- Add quick ladder drills
- Increase bound distance to 8 contacts
- Sprint distance: 25m

**Week 4 - Acceleration Builds**
- 3-point starts introduced
- Sprint distance: 30m
- Add resisted sprints (band optional)

**Week 5 - Speed Endurance**
- Sprint sets: 4 × 40m
- Reduced rest (2 min)
- Volume peak week

**Week 6 - Testing & Consolidation**
- 20m time trial (baseline)
- Technique review drills
- Deload volume 50%

---

## Progression Milestones

| Week | Milestone | Trigger |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 2 | Increase distance | 3 sessions rated "easy/good" |
| 4 | Add starts variant | Bound form consistent |
| 6 | Time trial | Program completion |

## Contact Limits (Beginner)

- Week 1-2: Max 50 contacts/week
- Week 3-4: Max 60 contacts/week
- Week 5-6: Max 70 contacts/week
