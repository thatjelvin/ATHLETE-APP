# Vertical Jump Builder Program

**Duration:** 8 weeks  
**Sessions/Week:** 3  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Equipment:** Minimal (boxes, bands optional)  
**Goal:** Increase vertical jump through power, strength, and reactive training

---

## Week Overview

| Week | Theme | Focus |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Foundation | Movement patterns, activation |
| 2 | Strength Base | Eccentric control, single-leg |
| 3 | Power Introduction | Low plyos, jump form |
| 4 | Reactive Development | Ground contact time |
| 5 | Strength-Power | Complex training |
| 6 | Peak Power | Higher intensity plyos |
| 7 | Speed-Strength | Reactive emphasis |
| 8 | Testing & Taper | Jump testing, deload |

---

## Week 1: Foundation

### Session A - Activation & Patterns (~35 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**
- Glute activation circuit
- Ankle mobility

**Activation (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Glute bridges | 3 × 12 | 45s | Glute activation |
| Single-leg glute bridge | 2 × 8 each | 45s | Unilateral activation |

**Strength (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Goblet squat (or BW) | 3 × 10 | 60s | Pattern groove |
| RDL | 3 × 8 | 60s | Hip hinge |

**Plyo Prep (8 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Squat jumps (no jump) | 3 × 5 | 60s | Landing practice |
| Ankle pogos | 2 × 15 | 45s | Reactive prep |

---

### Session B - Movement Quality (~30 min)

**Warmup (6 min)**

**Movement (15 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Split squat | 3 × 8 each | 60s | Single-leg control |
| Single-leg RDL | 3 × 6 each | 60s | Balance, posterior |
| Calf raises | 3 × 15 | 45s | Ankle strength |

**Core (6 min)**
- Plank: 3 × 30s
- Dead bugs: 2 × 10 each

---

### Session C - Power Introduction (~35 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**

**Reactive (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Line hops | 2 × 20 | 45s | Quick feet |
| Squat jumps | 3 × 5 | 90s | Vertical force |

**Strength (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 6 each | 90s | Depth, strength |

**Power (8 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Low box jumps (12") | 3 × 5 | 2 min | Landing, takeoff |

---

## Week 4: Reactive Development (Sample)

### Session A - Reactive Power (~40 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**

**Reactive (15 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Ankle pogos | 2 × 25 | 45s | Ground contact speed |
| Hurdle hops | 3 × 5 | 90s | Reactive stiffness |
| Drop lands (18") | 3 × 4 | 90s | Absorb and stick |

**Strength (12 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 6 each (3s ecc) | 90s | Eccentric control |

**Power (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Box jumps (18") | 3 × 5 | 2 min | Height focus |

**Note:** Depth jumps only for those without meniscus flag.

---

## Week 8: Testing & Taper

### Session A - Jump Testing
- Standing vertical jump: 3 attempts
- Approach vertical: 3 attempts
- Record best

### Session B - Light Movement
- Mobility work
- Light activation
- 50% volume

### Session C - Consolidation
- Favorite exercises, light load
- Program complete celebration

---

## Progression Milestones

| Week | Milestone | Trigger |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 3 | Add box jumps | Landing form solid |
| 5 | Increase box height +6" | 3 sessions "easy/good" |
| 6 | Add depth drops | No meniscus flag, form solid |
| 8 | Jump test | Program completion |

---

## Contact Limits (Intermediate)

- Week 1-2: Max 80 contacts/week
- Week 3-4: Max 100 contacts/week
- Week 5-6: Max 120 contacts/week
- Week 7-8: Max 100 contacts/week (taper)

---

## Meniscus Modifications

If `injuryFlag == "meniscus"`:
- No depth jumps
- Box height max 12"
- Contact limit reduced 25%
- Landing emphasis on soft absorption
