# Explosive Athlete Microprogram

**Duration:** 4 weeks  
**Sessions/Week:** 3-4  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Equipment:** Minimal  
**Goal:** Total body explosive power for multi-sport transfer

---

## Week Overview

| Week | Theme | Intensity |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Power Foundation | Medium |
| 2 | Explosive Development | Medium-High |
| 3 | Peak Power | High |
| 4 | Integration & Test | Medium (taper) |

---

## Week 1: Power Foundation

### Session A - Lower Body Power (~35 min)

**Warmup (6 min)**
- Dynamic stretches
- Glute activation

**Strength-Power (15 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Jump squats | 4 × 5 | 90s | Vertical force |
| Split squat jumps | 3 × 4 each | 90s | Unilateral power |

**Plyo (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Broad jumps | 4 × 4 | 2 min | Horizontal power |
| Lateral bounds | 3 × 5 each | 90s | Lateral power |

**Cooldown (4 min)**

---

### Session B - Upper Body & Core Power (~30 min)

**Warmup (5 min)**

**Power (15 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Medicine ball chest pass | 4 × 6 | 60s | Push power |
| Med ball slam | 4 × 6 | 60s | Total body |
| Rotational throws | 3 × 5 each | 60s | Rotational power |

**Core (8 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Plank with reach | 3 × 8 each | 45s | Anti-rotation |
| V-ups | 3 × 10 | 45s | Explosive core |

---

### Session C - Total Body Explosive (~40 min)

**Warmup (8 min)**

**Complex (20 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Box jumps | 4 × 4 | 90s | Vertical |
| Bounds | 4 × 6 | 90s | Horizontal |
| Med ball throw | 4 × 5 | 60s | Upper power |

**Sprint Work (10 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| 20m accelerations | 4 × | 2 min | Speed transfer |

---

## Week 3: Peak Power (Sample)

### Session A - Max Power Day

**Power Complex (25 min)**
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Intent |
|----------|-------------|------|--------|
| Depth jump to jump | 4 × 3 | 3 min | Reactive power |
| Single-leg bounds | 4 × 4 each | 2 min | Unilateral |
| Sprint 30m | 4 × | 3 min | Speed expression |

**Note:** Depth jumps for advanced/intermediate only, no meniscus flag.

---

## Week 4: Integration & Test

### Session A - Power Test
- Standing broad jump: 3 attempts
- Med ball throw: 3 attempts
- 20m sprint: 2 attempts

### Session B - Light Integration
- 50% volume
- Favorite movements
- Active recovery focus

---

## Progression Rules

| Week | Trigger | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| 2 | 3 "easy/good" sessions | Increase jump reps +1 |
| 3 | Form solid | Add advanced variations |
| 4 | Program complete | Record test metrics |

---

## Contact Limits

- Week 1: 80 contacts max
- Week 2: 100 contacts max
- Week 3: 120 contacts max
- Week 4: 60 contacts (taper)

---

## Exercise Substitutions

| If No Access To | Substitute |
|-----------------|------------|
| Medicine ball | Explosive push-ups, band throws |
| Box | Tuck jumps, squat jumps |
| Space for bounds | Vertical jumps, pogos |
